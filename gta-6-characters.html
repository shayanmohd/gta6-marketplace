The Cast

GTA 6 Characters: Lucia & Jason

For the first time in a mainline Grand Theft Auto, the story is built around two protagonists in a relationship — a modern Bonnie & Clyde. Here's everything confirmed about GTA 6's leads.

Protagonist

Lucia Caminos

The series' first major, non-optional female protagonist. Lucia grew up in Liberty City before ending up in Leonida, where she served time at the Leonida Penitentiary for a crime tied to "fighting for her family." Sharp, loyal, and done playing by the rules.

Protagonist

Jason Duval

An Army veteran who fell in with local drug-runners in the Leonida Keys. Jason grew up in the state and has deep ties to its criminal ecosystem — biker gangs, drug operations, and the hustle of Vice City's underworld.

A criminal love story

Rockstar has framed Lucia and Jason as partners in life and in crime — the narrative centers on their relationship as they try to make it in Leonida. In solo play you can switch between the two (press down on the D-pad in free roam), and heists feature contextual real-time switching between them.

The supporting cast

Beyond the leads, Rockstar has teased a colorful roster of Leonida characters — from social-media hustlers and strip-club figures to conspiracy-obsessed locals — painting a satirical picture of modern Florida. Expect the studio's trademark cast of allies, rivals, and wildcards to be revealed in the run-up to launch. We'll update this page as each is officially confirmed.

Who are the GTA 6 protagonists?

Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval — the first mainline GTA with two playable leads in a relationship.

Can you play as Lucia?

Yes — Lucia is fully playable and the series' first non-optional female protagonist. You can switch between her and Jason in solo play.

Is there a female protagonist in GTA 6?

Yes, Lucia Caminos — a first for the mainline series.
