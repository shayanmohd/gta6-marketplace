What's the best controller for GTA 6?

The standard DualSense (PS5) is our pick for most players thanks to haptics and adaptive triggers. Pro pads add paddles and remapping for competitive play.

Do I need a pro controller?

No — the standard pad is plenty. Pro controllers are a nice upgrade for GTA Online, not a requirement.
