What's the difference between the two editions?

Standard is the full base game. Ultimate adds bonus in-game content, currency, and a free month of GTA+ on digital pre-orders. Both get the Vintage Vice City Pack when pre-ordered before Nov 20.

Is the Ultimate Edition worth $20 more?

For heavy GTA Online players, yes — the bonus currency and GTA+ month offset the cost. For story-only players, the Standard Edition is enough.

Does GTA 6 come with a disc?

The physical version is a code in a box, not a disc. Both physical and digital deliver the same game.
