What to look for

  • Comfort first — you'll wear it for hours; clamp force and earcup padding matter most.
  • Platform match — PS5 3D audio support, or cross-platform if you switch devices.
  • Decent mic — if you'll play GTA Online with friends, mic clarity counts.
  • Wired vs wireless — wireless for freedom, wired for zero-latency and a lower price.
What's the best headset for GTA 6?

On PS5, a 3D-audio wireless set like the Pulse Elite. Cross-platform picks from Razer, SteelSeries, and HyperX are excellent for Xbox/PC, and budget wired headsets still sound great.

Is a headset worth it for GTA 6?

Definitely — GTA's world is built on ambient sound, and in GTA Online, hearing enemies is a real advantage.
