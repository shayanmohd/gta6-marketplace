# 🚀 GTA6.LLC — Week 1 Marketing Playbook

Your goal this week: **first traffic + first Amazon sales** (you need 3 in 180 days) while AdSense reviews. Two channels do the heavy lifting: **short-form video** (you control distribution) and **Reddit** (huge but strict). Do the videos daily; do Reddit carefully.

---

## ⚠️ Read this before you post on Reddit
r/GTA6 (2M+ members) and r/GTA are **strict about self-promotion**. Dropping your link in a post = removed + possible ban. The rule of thumb is **9:1** (nine genuine contributions for every one that mentions your site). So the Reddit plan below is **value-first**: you build reputation and brand recognition, and the link goes in your **profile/bio** and the **occasional comment where it genuinely helps** — not in post bodies on the big subs.

**Warm up your account first (Day 0):** make sure your Reddit account is a few weeks old with some normal comment karma. Brand-new accounts posting links get auto-filtered. Spend 2–3 days just commenting genuinely on GTA threads before posting.

**Where a link IS usually fine:** your own Reddit profile, smaller/newer GTA subs, and replies when someone literally asks "is there a countdown?" / "when does it come out?" — then linking gta6.llc is helpful, not spam.

## 📅 7 Reddit posts (value-first — no link in the body on big subs)

> Post to **r/GTA6** unless noted. Post 1×/day max. Engage with every comment for the first 2 hours (the algorithm rewards it).

**Day 1 — Discussion hook**
- **Title:** "We're officially under [X] days until GTA 6. What's the ONE thing you're doing first when it unlocks?"
- **Body:** "For me it's just driving down the Vice City strip at night with the radio on before I touch a single mission. No chaos, no cops — just vibes. What's your day-one ritual?"
- *Why:* pure engagement bait, zero promo, builds your account. (Update [X] with the live number from your own site.)

**Day 2 — Useful OC (screenshot, no link)**
- **Title:** "Made a clean countdown to launch to stop myself refreshing the Rockstar page 40× a day"
- **Body:** Post a **screenshot** of your site's countdown (not a link). If someone asks "where's this from?" → reply with the link in a comment. That's allowed and converts.

**Day 3 — Question that helps buyers (r/GTAOnline or r/GTA6)**
- **Title:** "Standard vs Ultimate edition — which did you pre-order and why?"
- **Body:** Share the actual differences ($79.99 vs $99.99, the Vintage Vice City Pack, the GTA+ month) as genuine info. In a **comment**, if useful: "I wrote up the full breakdown if anyone wants it."

**Day 4 — Nostalgia/lore**
- **Title:** "Lucia is the first female lead in mainline GTA history — how do you think the dual-protagonist story will actually work?"
- **Body:** Talk about the Bonnie & Clyde framing, the D-pad character switch, heist switching. Genuine discussion.

**Day 5 — Map hype**
- **Title:** "The GTA 6 map is reportedly ~2× GTA 5 with 700+ interiors. Which Leonida region are you most hyped to explore?"
- **Body:** Name the regions (Vice City, the Keys, Grassrivers, Mount Kalaga…). Discussion gold.

**Day 6 — Poll**
- **Title:** "Be honest — are you taking Nov 19–20 off work/school for GTA 6?" (use Reddit's poll feature)
- *Why:* massive engagement, zero promo, gets your name seen.

**Day 7 — Helpful roundup (smaller sub or your profile)**
- **Title:** "Everything confirmed about GTA 6 so far (release date, price, map, characters) — one place"
- **Body:** Write the actual summary in the post (don't just link). At the end: "Keeping this updated on my countdown site if useful — link in profile." (Softest possible CTA; only where the sub allows it.)

**Golden rules:** never post the same thing to multiple subs (spam filter), always reply to commenters, and put **gta6.llc in your Reddit profile bio** so anyone who checks you out finds it.

## 📌 Setup checklist (do once)
- [ ] Put **gta6.llc** in your TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and Reddit bios.
- [ ] Fill in `config.js → social` with your handles so the site links back to you.
- [ ] Set up a **Discord** invite (even a basic server) → put it in the site header + bios. A community you own = repeat traffic you don't rent from an algorithm.
- [ ] Turn on Google Analytics so you can see what's working.

**The one rule that matters:** consistency beats perfection. One rough video a day for 30 days will out-perform one "perfect" video a week. The countdown gives you infinite content — every single day the number changes.
