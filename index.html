Everything We Know

GTA 6 — Confirmed So Far

Only what Rockstar has officially shown or stated. Updated as new info drops.

Release

Out November 19, 2026

Rockstar's official date is Nov 19, 2026 on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. A PC version is expected later but hasn't been dated.

Story

Lucia & Jason — a criminal romance

The first mainline GTA built around two protagonists in a relationship. Lucia Caminos (the series' first non-optional female lead, from Liberty City) and Jason Duval, an Army vet turned Leonida Keys hustler.

Map

The state of Leonida

A Florida-inspired world roughly twice the size of GTA 5 — six biomes and 700+ enterable interiors spanning Vice City, the Keys, and the Everglades-like Grassrivers.

Gameplay

Swap characters on the fly

Press down on the D-pad to switch between Lucia and Jason in free roam. Heists feature contextual real-time switching between the two.

Trailers

Trailer 2 broke records

The second trailer (May 6, 2026) pulled 60M+ views in 24 hours — one of the biggest launches in trailer history.

Editions

Standard $79.99 · Ultimate $99.99

Two editions only — no separate collector's box. Pre-order before Nov 20 for the Vintage Vice City Pack; digital pre-orders add a free month of GTA+.

Quick Answers

GTA 6 FAQ

When does GTA 6 come out?

November 19, 2026 on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. PC is expected later, with no confirmed date yet.

How much does GTA 6 cost?

The Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99 (USD; regional prices vary).

Can I pre-order GTA 6 right now?

Yes — pre-orders opened June 25, 2026. Use the pre-order guide above to pick a store. Order before Nov 20 for the Vintage Vice City Pack.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Rockstar has said a PC version is expected, but it has not been dated. Historically PC releases have followed console launches.

Who are the main characters?

Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval — the first mainline GTA to center on two protagonists in a relationship. Lucia is the series' first non-optional female lead.

Where is GTA 6 set?

The fictional state of Leonida (Florida-inspired), centered on Vice City, plus the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.
