The GTA 6 Map: Welcome to Leonida

GTA 6 is set in the fictional state of Leonida — Rockstar's take on Florida — anchored by a modern Vice City. It's the studio's most ambitious world yet: reportedly about twice the size of GTA 5, with six biomes and 700+ enterable interiors.

How big is it vs GTA 5?

Reports point to a map around twice the size of GTA 5's Los Santos and Blaine County — but the bigger leap is density: hundreds of enterable buildings and far more interactive interiors mean the world feels much fuller to explore, not just larger.

How big is the GTA 6 map?

Roughly twice the size of GTA 5's, with six biomes and 700+ enterable interiors across Leonida.

Where is GTA 6 set?

In the fictional Florida-inspired state of Leonida — Vice City plus the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.

Is Vice City in GTA 6?

Yes — Vice City is the central city, sitting within the wider state of Leonida.
