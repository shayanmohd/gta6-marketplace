Does a VPN reduce lag in GTA Online?

It can, if your ISP routes gaming traffic poorly or throttles it. Connect to a server geographically close to you for the best result. It's not magic — a good baseline connection still matters.

Can a VPN stop DDoS attacks?

Yes. Because GTA Online uses peer hosting, other players can see your IP. A VPN hides it, so DDoS attacks hit the VPN server, not your home line.

Is using a VPN against Rockstar's rules?

Using one for privacy and DDoS protection is fine. Don't use it to cheat or exploit regional pricing — that can get you banned.
